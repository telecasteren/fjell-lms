# Course management (authors)

## Create a course

As an **Author**, go to **Courses** and use **Create course**. Enter details, then open **Manage** to build structure.

## Add modules and lessons

Inside course management:

- Add **modules** and order them.
- Add **lessons** under each module. Use **Edit** to change titles and content.

### Lesson editor

- **Content** tab — Title, content type (Text, SCORM, Multimedia, depending on availability), and body or uploads.
- **Quiz** tab — Add questions, options, and correct answers. Learners typically need **about 70%** correct to pass.

## Publishing and archiving

- **Draft** — Work in progress; not for general learners.
- **Published** — Available to eligible users.
- **Archived** — Hidden from typical learner views; authors may still see or manage as configured.

You can **delete** a course as an author; that removes related modules, lessons, and quizzes.

## Dashboard progress display

For learners, the **dashboard** may show:

- **Ongoing courses** — Started but not finished.
- **Current focus** — Often the ongoing course with the **highest completion %** so they can continue quickly.

Example: if three courses are partially done, all three may list as ongoing; the one closest to done may be highlighted as the main “continue” course.
